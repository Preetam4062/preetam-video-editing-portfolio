How I Work

The Editing Process

01

Discovery

We discuss your vision, visual styling, target audience, references, goals, and script outline.

02

Planning

Sorting footage, indexing timelines, storyboarding dynamic flow, and selecting backing music tracks.

03

Rough Cut

Piecing together the narrative structure, choosing the best takes, and pacing to match music rhythms.

04

Motion Graphics

Creating modern animated text overlays, titles, shape layers, call-outs, and VFX composite scenes.

05

Color Grading

Balancing exposure, color correction, and applying a cohesive luxury LUT grade for cinematic vibe.

06

Sound Design

Adding ambient soundscapes, foley clicks, swooshes, sub-hits, audio leveling, and vocal EQ enhancements.

07

Delivery

Final rendering in optimized web resolutions, exports for diverse aspect ratios, and file hand-off.
